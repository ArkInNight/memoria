# Recruitment (Gacha) System Mechanics

> Translator's note: "征集" is translated as **Recruitment/Pull**, "保底" as **pity/guarantee**, and in-game item/currency names (e.g. 独一律, 迷途之声, 秘闻之声) are translated descriptively for clarity.

---

## 1. Standard Banner Mechanics

In general, all characters follow the same base probabilities. For specific rules such as rate-up probabilities or pity, refer to the detailed rules of each banner.

**Base probability by rarity:**

| Rarity | Base Probability                                      |
| ------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 6-Star | 1.5% (combined probability including pity: **2.36%**) |
| 5-Star | 8.5%                                                  |
| 4-Star | 40%                                                   |
| 3-Star | 45%                                                   |
| 2-Star | 5%                                                    |

**Additional rules:**

- Every 10 pulls are **guaranteed** to include at least one 4-Star or higher character.
- If no 6-Star character is obtained within 60 consecutive pulls, the probability of getting a 6-Star on the next pull increases from 1.5% to **4%**. If still unsuccessful, the probability continues to increase by **2.5% per pull** thereafter.
- At a maximum of the **70th** pull, a 6-Star character is guaranteed (hard pity).
- After obtaining a 6-Star character, the probability resets back to the base value of 1.5%.

### Duplicate Character Conversion

Applies to all methods of obtaining characters (recruitment, bank exchange, system gifts, etc.). Characters already owned are converted based on the number of copies owned:

| Character Rarity | 2nd–6th duplicate converts to                            | 7th duplicate onward converts to  |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| ★★★★★★ (6-Star)  | 1 Character Portrait + 12x "Lost Voice (Large Capacity)" | 28x "Lost Voice (Large Capacity)" |
| ★★★★★ (5-Star)   | 1 Character Portrait + 4x "Lost Voice (Large Capacity)"  | 7x "Lost Voice (Large Capacity)"  |
| ★★★★ (4-Star)    | 1 Character Portrait + 8x "Lost Voice"                   | 12x "Lost Voice"                  |
| ★★★ (3-Star)     | 1 Character Portrait + 4x "Lost Voice"                   | 7x "Lost Voice"                   |
| ★★ (2-Star)      | 1 Character Portrait + 3x "Lost Voice"                   | 5x "Lost Voice"                   |

## 2. Limited-Time Character Banners

Split into two types:

- **Rerun Banner:** boosts the probability of previously released characters; the boosted character selection may change over time.
- **Event Banner:** boosts the probability of newly released characters in the current version; generally each version releases one banner per new character.

### Mechanics

- Pity is **shared** across banners of the same type, including between Rerun Banners and Event Banners.
- When a 6-Star character is obtained, there is a **50%** chance it is the current rate-up 6-Star character. If the 6-Star obtained is **not** the rate-up character, the next 6-Star obtained is **guaranteed** to be the rate-up character.
- When a 5-Star character is obtained, there is a **50%** chance it is one of the current rate-up 5-Star characters, with equal probability among all rate-up 5-Star characters.

## 3. Limited-Time Event Banners (限时活动征集)

Additional recruitment systems released during specific events. Pity is **not shared** with other recruitment types. Each series has its own specific mechanics, and generally boosts the probability of multiple characters within the same banner.

- **Ripples on the Water :** usually released alongside the start of a new version; characters that received a Rhapsody boost in the most recent version get boosted odds here.
- **Promise of the Water :** usually released during anniversary celebrations.
- **Boon of the Water :** usually released during Box Touring events.

### Ripples on the Water

- Players may freely choose 1 6-Star character from the available pool as the boosted-probability character, and may change the selection at any time.
- The first time a 6-Star character is obtained in this banner, there is a **100%** chance it is the chosen character (applies once only). After that, the chance of the chosen character becomes **50%**. If the 6-Star obtained is not the chosen character, the next 6-Star pull is **guaranteed** to be the chosen character.
- The first 10-pull in this event receives a 30% discount (70% of normal price) on the recruitment currency (独一律 / "Uniqueness Shard").
- "Lake's Ripple" falls under the **Limited-Time Event Banner** category and does not share pity with other recruitment types.
- Pity in this event is shared among "Lake's Ripple" banners specifically; after the event ends, the pity count is **not reset** and carries over to the next "Lake's Ripple" event.

### Invitation From the Lake

- The pull cap for this banner is 40; within 40 pulls, players are guaranteed at least one "Golden Thread Knot." After obtaining a "Golden Thread Knot," players can no longer pull on this banner.
- This banner does not grant 6-Star characters directly; opening the "Golden Thread Knot" allows the player to freely choose 1 6-Star character (see table above). The probability of obtaining each set of "Golden Thread Knot" is equal.
- The "Golden Thread Knot" disappears when the event ends, so it must be opened and the character selected before the event concludes.

### Covenant of the Water

- There is a pull cap of 60; after obtaining 2 "Golden Thread Groups," players can no longer pull on this banner.
- This banner does not grant 6-Star characters directly; within 30 pulls, players are guaranteed 1 random "Golden Thread Group" (either Group I or Group II), with a maximum of one of each type. Opening a "Golden Thread Group" allows the player to freely choose 1 of the 6-Star characters it contains (see table above).
- The "Golden Thread Group" disappears when the event ends, so it must be opened and the character selected before the event concludes.

### Promise of the Water

- Players may choose and lock in 1 6-Star character from the available pool as the boosted-probability character.
- When a 6-Star character is obtained in this banner, there is a **50%** chance it is the chosen character. If not, the next 6-Star pull is **guaranteed** to be the chosen character.
- "Lake's Promise" falls under the **Limited-Time Event Banner** category; its pity count accumulates independently and is not shared with other recruitment types.
- After the event ends, the pity count for "Lake's Promise" **resets to zero** and does not carry over to the same or other banner types.
- In this event, the first 10-pull only requires 5 "Uniqueness Shards" (独一律).

### Revelation of the Water

- Players may choose and lock in 2 6-Star characters from the available pool as boosted-probability characters.
- When a 6-Star character is obtained in this banner, there is a **70%** chance it is one of the 2 chosen characters. If not, the next 6-Star pull is **guaranteed** to be one of the chosen characters.
- "Lake's Revelation" falls under the **Limited-Time Event Banner** category; its pity count accumulates independently and is not shared with other recruitment types.
- After the event ends, the pity count **resets to zero** and does not carry over to the same or other banner types.
- In "Lake's Revelation 3" (released for Lunar New Year 2026, version 3.4), the first 10-pull only requires 5 "Uniqueness Shards."

### Boon of the Water

- Players may choose and lock in 3 6-Star characters from the available pool as boosted-probability characters.
- When a 6-Star character is obtained in this banner, there is a **100%** chance it is one of the 3 chosen characters, with equal probability among the three.
- "Lake's Gift" falls under the **Limited-Time Event Banner** category; its pity count accumulates independently and is not shared with other recruitment types.
- After the event ends, the pity count **resets to zero** and does not carry over to the same or other banner types.
- In this event, the first 10-pull only requires 5 "Uniqueness Shards."

### The Snow of Dushuo

- Players may choose and lock in 1 6-Star character from the available pool as the boosted-probability character.
- During the event period, daily login grants 1 free 10-pull, for up to 10 days total. Free daily 10-pulls **cannot be stacked or carried over**.
- "Dushuo Feiqiong" falls under the **Limited-Time Event Banner** category; its pity count accumulates independently and is not shared with other recruitment types.
- After the event ends, the pity count **resets to zero** and does not carry over to the same or other banner types.

## 4. Exclusive/Limited Banners

Released during anniversary celebrations, Box Touring events, and Lunar New Year events. Characters from exclusive banners **do not** join the standard banner pool, and can only be re-obtained when the corresponding exclusive banner reruns.

### Mechanics

- All types of **Exclusive Banners** (including Lunar New Year Exclusive, Anniversary Exclusive, Box Touring Exclusive, and Exclusive Reruns) accumulate pity **independently**, and it is **not shared** with other recruitment types.
- After the event ends, the pity count **resets to zero** and does not carry over to the same or other banner types.
- A character's first-ever Exclusive Banner appearance generates **"Lore Voice" (秘闻之声)**, usable in the **[Lore Identification]** menu to additionally exchange for the current rate-up character and insight materials. Each pull earns 1 "Lore Voice." This item has a **time-limited use window** — once the [Lore Identification] menu closes, "Lore Voice" becomes invalid, so it should be used promptly.
- Each event may have different mechanics; please refer to the specific notes for each event.

## 5. Collaboration Banners (联动征集)

Released during special collaboration versions. Characters from collaboration banners **do not** join the standard banner pool.

### Mechanics

- Pity for Collaboration Banners accumulates independently and is **not shared** with any other recruitment type.
- After the event ends, the pity count **resets to zero** and does not carry over to the same or other banner types.

### Special Mechanics

**"Prophet of the Era" & "Eagle Soaring Over the Sea of Wine" (Assassin's Creed Collaboration)**

- Within the first 10 pulls, a 5-Star or higher character is guaranteed.
- At the 100th, 160th, 220th, and 280th pulls, players receive an additional free copy of the collaboration rate-up character for that banner; this bonus can be obtained a maximum of 4 times.

## 6. Beginner & Standard Banners (新人 & 常驻征集)

### Mechanics

- **[Beginner Special Banner]:** Within 30 pulls, players are guaranteed 1 6-Star character. After 30 pulls, this banner is removed from the recruitment system and **cannot reappear**.
- **[Standard Character Banner]:** Pity mechanics are **independent** and not shared with other recruitment types.

## 7. Data Model & Reconciliation

The app encodes this document in `assets/banners.json` (one record per banner
edition/run) + `assets/banner-rules.json` (mechanics defaults per `type`).
See `src/lib/shared/types.ts` (`BannerType`, `PityPool`, `RateUpRule`) and
`tools/README.md` (Banner data model) for the full picture.

| Docs term (above)                    | `BannerType`        | Pity pool (resets)                       | Rate-up rule | Extra rules |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ------------ | ----------- |
| Event Banner (new character)         | `debut`             | `character` (**carries** globally)       | `5050`       | —           |
| Rerun Banner                         | `rerun`             | `character` (**carries** globally)       | `5050`       | —           |
| Exclusive/Limited Banner             | `exclusive`         | `exclusive` (resets per edition)         | `5050`       | —           |
| Collaboration Banner                 | `collaboration`     | `collaboration` (resets per edition)     | `5050`       | milestone copies at 100/160/220/280 |
| Ripples on the Water                 | `ripples`           | `ripples` (**carries** between editions) | `100-first`  | selector 1 |
| Promise of the Water / From the Lake's Heart / Abundance of the Water | `lake-promise`      | `lake-promise` (resets)                  | `5050`       | selector 1 |
| Revelation of the Water              | `lake-revelation`   | `lake-revelation` (resets)               | `7030`       | selector 2 |
| Boon of the Water                    | `lake-gift`         | `lake-gift` (resets)                     | `100`        | selector 3 |
| Invitation From the Lake             | `lake-invitation`   | `lake-invitation` (resets)               | `box`        | pull cap 40 |
| Covenant of the Water                | `lake-vow`          | `lake-vow` (resets)                      | `box`        | pull cap 60 |
| The Snow of Dushuo                   | `snow`              | `snow` (resets)                          | `5050`       | selector 1 |
| Yearning of the Water (湖的企盼)       | `yearning`          | `yearning` (resets)                      | `7030`       | selector 1 |
| Beginner Special Banner              | `beginner`          | `beginner` (**carries** — permanent)     | `none`       | pull cap 30 |
| Standard Character Banner            | `standard`          | `standard` (**carries** — permanent)     | `none`       | —           |

Pity grouping rule: a pull's pity group is `pityPool` when the banner
`carryOver`s (character & ripples carry; beginner & standard are permanent);
otherwise it is `pityPool + ':' + slug(name)-version`, i.e. each edition
resets its own counter.
